# Early Game Setup
<details>
  <summary>Get Phantom Armor</summary>
  All three pieces of the Phantom Set are located in Central Hyrule inside EX-marked metallic treasure chests. You will need your **Magnesis Rune** to pull each chest out of the dirt or water.

**1. Phantom Helmet**

* **Location:** Coliseum Ruins (Ground floor).
* **Exact Spot:** Buried in the ground against the northwest wall.
* **Quick Strategy:** Paraglide down directly from the top rim onto the northwest wall at ground level. You can grab the chest and warp out without triggering or fighting the Lynel in the center arena.

**2. Phantom Armor**

* **Location:** Sacred Ground Ruins (Directly south of Hyrule Castle).
* **Exact Spot:** Submerged in the water along the southeast side of the central stone ring.
* **Quick Strategy:** Warp to Central Tower or Katah Chuki Shrine and glide into the ceremonial grounds. Activate Magnesis to spot the glowing chest in the water and pull it to dry land.

**3. Phantom Greaves**

* **Location:** Hyrule Garrison Ruins (Southeast of Central Tower).
* **Exact Spot:** Buried in the mud next to an inert, decayed Guardian chassis on the eastern edge of the ruins.
* **Quick Strategy:** Glide southeast from Central Tower into the garrison grounds. Keep an eye out for patrolling active Guardians nearby, sweep the dirt with Magnesis, and pull the chest out.
</details>
