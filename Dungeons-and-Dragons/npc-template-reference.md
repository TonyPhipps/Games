# Size
(Affects speed, Hit Points)
Tiny, Small, Medium, Large, Huge

# Hit Points
Formula: SUM + (QuanityOfDice * ConstitutionModifier) = Hit Points
- Tiny: d4 (Average: 2.5)
- Small: d6 (Average: 3.5)
- Medium: d8 (Average: 4.5) — Used for 99% of humanoid NPCs
- Large: d10 (Average: 5.5)
- Huge: d12 (Average: 6.5)

# Speed
- Standard - 30ft
- Short-Legged - 25ft
- Swift - 35ft+
