For a stealth-focused Melee build in *Fallout: New Vegas*, the perk progression relies heavily on staggering, bypassing Damage Threshold (DT), hitting fast, and maximizing critical Sneak Attacks.

The primary progression path, split between core combat mechanics, stealth integration, and essential utility, maps out as follows:


# **Key Build Reminders**
* **Stat Checks:** Ensure you have at least **7 Agility** (or 6 + 1 from the Usanagi Medical Implant) to take *Slayer* at Level 24.
* **Unarmed Investment:** Even though this is a Melee build, **Unarmed 90** is mandatory to unlock both *Piercing Strike* and *Slayer*. Raise Unarmed early so you don't delay *Piercing Strike* at level 12.
* **Unstoppable Force Note:** While *Unstoppable Force* (STR 7, Melee 90) lets you bypass blocks, enemies block infrequently enough that it is lower priority than *Piercing Strike* or *Ninja*. You can grab it later in the DLC levels if desired.

# **Mandatory Melee Combat Perks**
* **Super Slam! (Level 8 | STR 6, Melee 45)**
* **Why:** Gives every hit a 15% chance (one-handed) or 30% chance (two-handed) to knock down enemies and make them ragdoll. It completely trivializes aggressive enemies like Deathclaws, Super Mutants, and Nightstalkers by locking them on the ground.

* **Piercing Strike (Level 12 | Unarmed 70)**
* **Why:** Reduces the target’s Damage Threshold (DT) by 15 for both Melee and Unarmed attacks. This is the single most important damage perk for melee, as heavily armored targets (Legion Legates, Brotherhood Paladins, Deathclaws) take virtually full damage. *Requires putting skill points into Unarmed early!*

* **Slayer (Level 24 | AGI 7, Unarmed 90)**
* **Why:** Increases the attack speed of all Melee and Unarmed weapons by 30%. Combined with fast weapons like Chance's Knife or the Katana, you can proc *Super Slam!* knockdowns near-instantaneously.


# **Stealth & Critical Synergies**

* **Ninja (Level 20 | Sneak 80, Melee 80)**
* **Why:** Gives a flat +15% Critical Hit Chance with Melee/Unarmed weapons and adds +25% damage to Sneak Attack Criticals. A cornerstone perk for a sneaky melee setup.

* **Better Criticals (Level 16 | PER 6, LCK 6)**
* **Why:** Increases Critical Damage by 50%. Since sneak attacks guaranteed a critical hit, this dramatically boosts your opener damage from stealth.

* **Silent Running (Level 12 | AGI 6, Sneak 50)**
* **Why:** Allows you to run while remaining in stealth without generating extra noise, eliminating the tedious slow-crouch crawl up to targets.

* **Light Touch (Level 2 | AGI 6, Repair 45 — *Dead Money DLC*)**
* **Why:** Gives +10% Critical Chance and inflicts -25% enemy critical chance while wearing light armor. Fits light sneak armors (like Joshua Graham’s Armor or Ulysses’ Duster).


## **3. Utility, Speed & Survivability**

* **Travel Light (Level 4 | Survival 45)**
* **Why:** Grants a +10% movement speed bonus when wearing light armor or no armor. Movement speed is vital for melee characters to close gaps quickly, even out of stealth.

* **Purifier (Level 14)**
* **Why:** Adds +66% Melee and Unarmed damage against abominations (Deathclaws, Centaurs, Nightstalkers, Spores, Tunnelers). Eliminates the hardest non-human threats in the game.

* **Stonewall (Level 8 | STR 6, END 6)**
* **Why:** Grants +5 DT against Melee/Unarmed attacks and makes you immune to being knocked down in combat. Prevents you from getting stun-locked by other melee users.

* **Jury Rigging (Level 14 | Repair 90)**
* **Why:** Allows you to repair high-tier unique melee weapons (like *Chance's Knife*, *Oh, Baby!*, or *Gehenna*) using cheap, common melee weapons (like Pool Cues or Lead Pipes).
